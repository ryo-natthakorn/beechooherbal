Charity Trip to Naan Dec 2019!

We are heading to Naan on the 27th of December 2019 to help the kids at the local school. Our Bee Choo Udomsuk manager, Bua, brought to our attention that her home province is still very much in need of support from the public as her home town is very poor.

Through Bua, we managed to get into contact with the school and ask them how we could assist them. The school has requested assistant in terms of donating school books, pencil boxes, study equipment and helping out with minor renovation for the children toilet and playground.

We’ve decided to hold a 1-day donation drive on the 14th of December 2019 in Bangkok, Thailand.

The participating outlets are:

1) Bee Choo Udomsuk2) Bee Choo Tawanna3) Bee Choo Siam Square One

All proceeds from treatments done on the 14th December 2019 will be used for charitable purposes. All proceeds will go towards the students. We humbly request your support! Bring your friends, aunties, uncles, mom, dad down to any of our outlets here in Bangkok on the 14th of December 2019 for a herbal scalp treatment. Whatever you pay will be donated to the kids!

If you are able to donate any study equipment, books, shelving, study desk etc, please email us at info@beechooherbal.com
