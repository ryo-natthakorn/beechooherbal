First outlet in the province, Chonburi!

Bee Choo Herbal Thailand is pleased to announce that we have opened our first branch outside Bangkok under the dealership of JnJ Herb Co., Ltd who also owns and operates Bee Choo Siam, Bee Choo Kallapaphruk and now Bee Choo Chonburi; brining out total outlets to 8 outlets across Thailand.

The Chonburi outlet generous in its spacing and high ceiling, giving customers a relaxing touch the moment they enter into the outlet. This outlet is well lited with custom made wooden mirrors that enhances the natural vibe to Bee Choo’s signature herbal treatment.
