Essence Shampoo - Signature look, brand new feel, more natural than ever

We are pleased to announce that our previous Anti-hair Loss Shampoo has been replaced by a newly formulated “Essence Shampoo” which will be retailing at THB 550.

At Bee Choo, we strive to bring to our customers products that have as little harmful chemicals, and none if possible. We are proud to say that the Essence Shampoo is totally free from: Mineral Oils, Paraben, SLES and Silicone. Furthermore, there is no animal-derived ingredients in the formula and no animals were involved in the testing of the product.

The Essence Shampoo can be used daily. It is a gentle cleanser with strengthening properties, it nourishes and rebalances the scalp pH level while promoting a healthy environment for hair growth. Enriched with the omega rich extracts of walnut, grapes and linseed, the fatty acid nourishes the follicles and strengthens the hair from root to tip, making it less prone to breakage. With the addition of peach extracts, it leaves your tresses soft and shiny after every wash.

Who is Essence Shampoo suitable for?

Recommended specially for users who have hair fall issues and/or sensitive scalp. The shampoo can be used daily or alternating with another shampoo depending on user preference.

Conditioning the hair, making it soft and smooth.

Omega-3 fats nourish the hair follicles and encourage growth. They also prevent inflammation, helping to stave off hair loss, dandruff, and other hair and scalp conditions.

Newly Formulated Essence Vitamins - Stronger, better, more natural

Similar to the Essence Shampoo, our Essence Vitamins is now totally free from: Mineral Oils, Paraben, SLES and Silicone. Furthermore, there is no animal-derived ingredients in the formula and no animals were involved in the testing of the product.

The Essence Vitamins is an intensive concentrate formulated with the well-known goodness of the omega rich extracts from walnut and grapes, it protects the scalp from free radicals, while nourishing the follicles and hair shaft, making it stronger. With the conditioning properties of peach extract, it helps prevent dryness while promoting a healthy environment for hair growth.

Who is Essence Vitamins Suitable for?

We recommended for customers who require an additional boost of vitamins for their scalp as well as customer experience hair fall issues. *It is best done immediately after treatment at our salons.
