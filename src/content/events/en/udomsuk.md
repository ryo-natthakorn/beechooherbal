Bee Choo Udomsuk which has been in operation since 14 July 2018 will be conducting its opening ceremony of Bee Choo Udomsuk on 23 Sept 2018. During this special day, there will be amazing promotions such as the limited time: 5 +1 free package and a special Bee Choo umbrella plus goodie bag will be out to all that attend.

Please come if you can and bring as many friends as you can!

There will be food, Thai dance, Lion Dance. So come and enjoy the event with Bee Choo Thailand! We welcome all our friends, family, and customers from any branches.
