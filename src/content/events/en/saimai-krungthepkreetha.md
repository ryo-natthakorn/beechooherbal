Bee Choo Tawanna’s branch has relocated to Sai Mai from 5th Sept with grand opening scheduled on 11th Sept 2022 as the auspicious day. The management team of Sai Mai is the same as the Tawanna branch. The relocation was decided after Tawanna’s location was deeemed to be inadequate.

Customers of Bee Choo Tawanna can choose to patron nearby new outlet Bee Choo Krungthep Kreetha (opened 10th Sep 2022).

With the opening of Sai Mai and Krungthep Kreetha, Bee Choo Thailand has 7 outlets as of Sep 2022, 5 years since Mr. Crispin Francis took charge of the Bee Choo franchise in the kingdom of Thailand.
